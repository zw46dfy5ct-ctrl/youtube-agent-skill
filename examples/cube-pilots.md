# Три пилота по 36 секунд
Общее начало каждого английского промпта: Vertical 9:16, matte ceramic cubes, graphite floor, soft side light, locked three-quarter camera, continuous action, no text, silent. Каждый ряд — отдельная сцена. При сборке использовать последний кадр предыдущей сцены как референс.

| Секунды | Кубик против потока | Мост с дырой | Флаг из кубиков |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | A red cube slides upstream between ivory cubes. Вариант B: два ряда расходятся перед красным кубиком. | A rolling blue cube approaches a one-cube gap. B: показать пустоту сквозь пролёт. | A corner of the tiled US flag peels up. B: первым падает синий угловой кубик. |
| 2–10 | The red cube nudges a single row sideways. | A loose tile slides toward the gap one row below. | Red and white stripes fold into parallel staircases. |
| 10–18 | The shifted row tips the next row like dominoes. | The rolling cube tips a lever at the bridge entrance. | The blue corner tumbles down the striped stairs. |
| 18–26 | A diagonal wave crosses the entire grid. | The lever lifts the loose tile into the bridge deck. | The falling blue cube tips the bottom step. |
| 26–34 | The wave opens a red arrow pointing upstream. | The blue cube rolls across the newly filled gap. | The stairs flatten in a wave, revealing the complete flag. |
| 34–36 | Push into the arrow tip, match cut to the red cube. | Follow the cube to an identical gap for the loop. | Match the camera to the opening corner as it lifts. |
